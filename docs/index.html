Privacy

Idle's privacy policy.

Last updated: May 12, 2026.

Idle is a quiet little app for the five things you actually want to get done today. We built it to help you focus, not to study you. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect.

Two things, and only two things:

  • Your email address. We use it to sign you in and to send password-reset and email-confirmation links. We do not send marketing emails. We do not share your email with anyone.
  • Your tasks. The things you type into Idle — task text, your "why," when you added or finished them, and which ones you refused or burned. We store this so it syncs across your devices and so you don't lose anything if you reinstall.

What we don't collect.

  • No location.
  • No contacts.
  • No photos.
  • No advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party trackers.
  • No "behavioral data," scroll heatmaps, or session recordings.

Where your data lives.

Your tasks and email are stored on our database provider, Supabase. Supabase hosts the data on Amazon Web Services. Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

The app also keeps a copy of your tasks on your phone so it works offline and feels instant. That copy is removed when you uninstall the app.

Notifications.

Idle pushes nothing by default. The only notification is an optional Morning bell you can turn on in Rules. It's scheduled by your phone, not by us. We don't see it, we don't trigger it, and we don't know whether you have it on.

Who we share data with.

Nobody. We don't sell, rent, trade, or share your data. The only exceptions are:

  • Our database provider (Supabase), because that's where the data is stored.
  • If a law requires it — for example, a valid court order. We'd push back on anything that looked like a fishing expedition.

Your rights.

  • See your data. Email us and we'll send you a copy of everything we have.
  • Delete your data. Email us and we'll delete your account and every task you ever added. This is permanent.
  • Correct your data. You can edit anything inside the app yourself.

Children.

Idle is not designed for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Changes.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for any meaningful change, we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect.
